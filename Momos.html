Grandma's Secret Momos Recipie

This tea recipe has been in my family for generations and never fails to delight.

Ingredients

  • For the outer covering (dough),Maida (all-purpose flour) – 2 cups,Salt – a pinch,Water – as needed (to knead a firm dough)
  • For the filling (Veg version),Cabbage – finely chopped,Carrot – grated or finely chopped,Onion / Spring onion – finely chopped,Capsicum (optional) – finely chopped,Garlic – finely minced,Ginger – finely minced,Green chilli – finely chopped (optional),Soy sauce – 1–2 tsp,Black pepper – to taste,Salt – to taste,Oil – 1 tsp

Instructions

  1. Knead the dough
  2. Prepare the filling
  3. Roll the dough into small discs
  4. Fill and shape the momos
  5. Steam the momos
  6. Serve hot with chutney

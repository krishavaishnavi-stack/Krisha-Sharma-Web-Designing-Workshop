Grandma's Secret Tea Recipie

This tea recipe has been in my family for generations and never fails to delight.

Ingredients

  • Water,Milk,Tea leaves (chai patti),Sugar,Fresh ginger (crushed),Cardamom (elaichi) — optional but very common
  • Cloves (1–2),Cinnamon (small piece),Black pepper (1–2 crushed)
  • Optional: a few strands of saffron or a pinch of nutmeg

Instructions

  1. Take a pan and add water.
  2. Add crushed ginger, cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, and black pepper
  3. Boil for 1–2 minutes until the spices release aroma.
  4. Add tea leaves and boil till the water turns dark.
  5. Pour in milk and add sugar as per taste.
  6. Let the tea boil on low flame for 3–4 minutes.
  7. (Optional) Add saffron or nutmeg at the end.
